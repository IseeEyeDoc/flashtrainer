# Flash Trainer

Free software to go with the book *The Invisible Step Before Reading Begins*
by Cled T. Click, O.D.

**Open Flash Trainer:** https://iseeeyedoc.github.io/flashtrainer/

Flash Trainer shows letters, numbers, shapes, and words for a short time, and the
child tells you what they saw. It helps children practice correct order, visual
memory, a wider view, and direction.

- Book Mode for parents and teachers, Advanced Mode for more advanced practice
- Built-in help, guided tour, and read-aloud
- Instructions in English and Spanish (training letters and words are always English)
- Works offline: save the page to your computer and open it without internet
- No names or results are collected

Flash Trainer is a practice tool. It is not a test, and it does not diagnose or
treat any reading or vision problem.
